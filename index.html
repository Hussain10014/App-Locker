How to Lock Android Screen

1
Enable Do Not Disturb:

Pull down notification bar and tap "Do Not Disturb" to hide all notification popups.

2
Android App Pinning (Screen Pinning):

Swipe up to view Recent Apps → Tap the app icon above the card → Tap "Pin this app".

This locks the screen inside ZenLock and disables the Home and notification bar!
